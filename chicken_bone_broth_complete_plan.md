# Chicken Bone Broth Powder & Collagen Peptides — Complete India Business Plan

> **Your starting point:** Raw chicken bone broth powder from China at $12/kg (~₹1,020/kg). Sample received — tastes bland. Plan: dry-blend with Indian spices in India, pack in 15g sachets (20-25 per box), sell in India.
>
> **For every process below:** 3 approaches — **DIY** (do it yourself) | **Outsource** (pay someone) | **Smart Move** (best for a new company)

## 2. FSSAI Licensing (Importer + Manufacturer)

| | DIY | Outsource | Smart Move |
|--|-----|-----------|------------|
| **What** | Apply yourself on FoSCoS portal; prepare Form B, layout plan, food safety docs | Hire an FSSAI regulatory consultant to handle application + inspection | Hybrid: consultant advises, you file |
| **Cost** | ₹7,500–₹15,000/year (just govt fees) | ₹50,000–₹2,00,000 (consultant + govt fees) | ₹30,000–₹80,000 (consultant for guidance only + you file) |
| **Time** | 60–120 days (likely rejections/queries if first time) | 45–75 days (consultant knows what inspectors want) | 50–90 days |
| **Effort** | Very high — need to prepare factory layout, recall plan, FSMS documentation | Low — consultant prepares everything | Medium — you learn the system with expert hand-holding |
| **Risk** | High rejection risk if documents are incomplete | Low — consultants have relationships with FSSAI offices | Low-medium |

**Smart Move:** **Hire a consultant for the first license only** (₹30K–₹80K). They'll prepare your documentation templates (recall plan, FSMS, layout) which you can reuse for renewals yourself. Ask them: "Does chicken bone broth powder need Proprietary Food approval?" — this answer alone is worth the consultant fee.

**You need TWO FSSAI activities covered:**
| License | Why | Fee | Timeline |
|---------|-----|-----|----------|
| **FSSAI Central License (Importer)** | Mandatory for ALL food importers regardless of turnover | ₹7,500/year | 45–90 days |
| **FSSAI State/Central License (Manufacturer)** | For your blending + packing facility | ₹2,000–₹7,500/year | 45–90 days |

**Apply via:** FoSCoS Portal — https://foscos.fssai.gov.in

**Documents needed:** Form B application, blueprint/layout of processing unit, list of food products, business constitution proof, list of machinery, water quality report, recall plan, food safety management documentation.

**Who to hire:** Search "FSSAI consultant" on LinkedIn; or firms like Food Research Lab (Mysore), AQCS India, or local food safety consultants.

### POTENTIAL BLOCKER: Proprietary Food Approval
Chicken bone broth powder does NOT have a specific FSSAI standard. This may require **Proprietary Food approval** under Regulation 2.12.1.
- Timeline: **6–18 months** (runs parallel with other setup)
- Cost: ₹5,000–₹10,000 application fee + consultant fees
- **This is your #1 blocker. Confirm with consultant in Week 1.**

## 6. Customs Brokerage & HS Code Classification

| | DIY | Outsource | Smart Move |
|--|-----|-----------|------------|
| **What** | Learn ICEGATE portal, file Bill of Entry yourself | Hire a licensed Customs House Agent (CHA) | **Always outsource to CHA** |
| **Cost** | ₹0 (but mistakes cost lakhs) | ₹5,000–₹15,000 per consignment | ₹5,000–₹15,000 per consignment |
| **Time** | Days of learning + high error risk | Same-day to 3 days | Same as outsource |
| **Effort** | Extremely high | Zero | Low — broker does it, you watch and learn |

**Smart Move:** **Always outsource customs to a CHA. No exception.** One wrong HS code costs you lakhs in extra duty or your shipment gets stuck. Find a CHA who specializes in **food imports**.

**Key instruction to CHA:** "Find me the HS code that gives the lowest legitimate duty rate. Give me the exact landed cost breakdown before I place my first order."

**Most likely HS Codes:**
| HS Code | Description | Estimated Total Duty |
|---------|-------------|---------------------|
| **1603.00** | Extracts and juices of meat | 30–60% total |
| **2106.90** | Food preparations not elsewhere specified | 50–100%+ total |
| **3504.00** | Peptones and derivatives (for collagen peptides) | 30–50% |

**Duty structure breakdown (HS 2106.90 example):**
| Duty Component | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| Basic Customs Duty (BCD) | 30–50% |
| Social Welfare Surcharge | 10% of BCD |
| IGST (on import) | 18% |
| Agriculture Infrastructure Cess | Variable (0–100%) |
| **Total landed cost multiplier** | **1.5x – 2.5x of CIF price** |

> **CRITICAL**: At $12/kg CIF (~₹1,020/kg), your landed cost after duties = **₹1,530 – ₹2,550/kg**. This makes or breaks your unit economics.
